About

Data and insight analyst with two years and eight months of continuous commercial experience, from September 2020 to May 2023. BI analysis at Confused.com, data management at CeRID in Nairobi, and independent consulting through Tech&City, the company I co-founded.

I work in transport operations now, so I have spent the last two years on the side of the data where it actually gets produced. Depots, handheld devices, tachograph records, load sheets. That is useful when the question is why the numbers look the way they do, because I have been the person whose week changes depending on what a report says.

What I am good at is the join between the two. Taking a messy question from someone operational, working out what they are actually trying to decide, then getting them a number that answers it rather than a table that describes it.

I build with AI assistance as a deliberate working method, and I can explain every query and design decision in anything on this page.
